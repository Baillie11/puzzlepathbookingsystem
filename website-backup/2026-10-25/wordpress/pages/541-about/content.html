About Puzzle Path

What Is Puzzle Path?

Puzzle Path is an interactive scavenger hunt experience designed to help you explore the Gold Coast in the most exciting way possible—through puzzles, clues, and real-world discovery.
Whether you're a local looking for a fresh adventure or a tourist wanting to see the city from a new angle, our quests combine fun, challenge, and discovery in every step.

Our Mission: Adventure for Everyone

We believe adventure should be accessible to everyone. That’s why we design our quests to be inclusive, family-friendly, and flexible for all ages and abilities. Puzzle Path is here to bring people together through the power of play.

How Puzzle Path Works

  • Choose Your Quest - Browse themed adventures across the Gold Coast, from beaches to rainforests.
  • Follow the Clues - Each checkpoint reveals riddles and local trivia.
  • Uncover Secrets - Unlock local history, hidden gems, and fun surprises.

Born on the Gold Coast

Puzzle Path began with a single idea: to help someone experience joy through adventure.

The very first scavenger hunt was created by Andrew, the original creator and director of Puzzle Path, for a client living with a disability through the NDIS. They had such a great time exploring, solving clues, and sharing the experience, it sparked a bigger vision:

"What if everyone could access this kind of fun—personal, inclusive, and filled with discovery?"

That question became the heart of Puzzle Path.

It’s your turn to uncover the Gold Coast, one clue at a time.
