Privacy and your visit

This landing page does not request customer details or use tracking cookies. Your campaign invitation stays in the page and booking links.

Optional aggregate measurement records the campaign, partner, selected quest, event type and time. It is disabled in this local preview. When enabled, it respects Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. It does not record IP addresses, email addresses or complete referring URLs. Hosting providers may keep their own operational access logs.

Booking details and payment information are handled separately by the existing Puzzle Path booking service. A click is never counted as a completed booking.
